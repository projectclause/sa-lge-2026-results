A turning point for Mandela’s party

Many foreign pieces treat the result as a turning point for the ANC. The New York Times’ John Eligon wrote that South Africa’s politics may never be the same again. The Economist welcomed voters giving up on the party, AP explained how it lost its way, and the FT reported on townships turning against it.

The angle abroad

Most foreign pieces open with Nelson Mandela. They treat the result as the end of an era for a liberation movement that readers abroad still associate with 1994, more than as a municipal election.

Where they disagree

The tone varies. The Economist welcomes the decline as good for South Africa, while AP and the FT look at what drove voters away, including anger in the townships.
