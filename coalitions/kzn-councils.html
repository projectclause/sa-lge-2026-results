The ANC partners MK in uMhlathuze and uPhongolo, and the DA in KwaDukuza

EWN reports an MK Party and ANC coalition has ousted the IFP in uMhlathuze, and Central News South Africa reports the two parties have taken control of uPhongolo. In KwaDukuza, The Witness reports the ANC and DA have partnered to keep the MK Party out of government.
