MK’s Nomathamsanqa Mthembu becomes uMhlathuze mayor as the ANC, DA and IFP take KwaDukuza

IOL, News24 and SABC News report the MK Party’s Nomathamsanqa Mthembu has been elected uMhlathuze mayor, with News24 saying an ANC-MK pact sidelined the IFP and DA. In KwaDukuza, The Citizen reports a last-minute deal has put the ANC, DA and IFP in control, and Central News South Africa reports the ANC and MK have taken uPhongolo.

Preamble’s AI summary of 9 reports. Updated 27 November 2026.

In brief

  • IOL, News24 and SABC News report the MK Party’s Nomathamsanqa Mthembu was elected uMhlathuze mayor; News24 says the ANC-MK pact sidelined the IFP and DA, and EWN earlier reported the coalition ousted the IFP.
  • Central News South Africa reports the ANC and MK Party formed a coalition to take control of uPhongolo Municipality.
  • The Citizen reports a last-minute coalition deal saw the ANC, DA and IFP take control of KwaDukuza, and The Witness reports the ANC and DA partnered to keep the MK Party out of government there.
  • IOL reports DA leadership will attend the uMngeni inauguration, which it describes as one of only 10 outright wins in KwaZulu-Natal.
