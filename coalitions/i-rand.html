International · Markets · The rand and investors

The rand slides as investors watch the talks

Financial outlets link the talks to the currency. Reuters reported the rand at a seven-month low on 17 November as traders tracked the coalition talks, and near a one-year low two days later. Bloomberg reported it testing R17 to the dollar with the focus on politics, and Michael Cohen wrote that the messy fallout had rattled investors.

The angle abroad

This is the part of the story covered almost only by foreign and financial outlets. They read the coalition talks as political risk, and track the talk of the ANC turning away from the DA through the rand.

What to watch

Wire and markets reporters link moves in the currency to news from the talks. A signed deal in Gauteng, or any sign of strain in the GNU, is likely to show up in their market reports first.
