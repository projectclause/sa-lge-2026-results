The ANC loses its grip on South Africa’s metros

News24’s columnists saw the ANC’s decline speeding up, and after the count it was left without control of a single metro.

Preamble’s AI summary of 20 reports. Updated 21 November 2026.

In brief

  • Du Toit expected the vote to confirm the ANC’s faster decline
  • The ANC risked falling to second, third or fourth place in seven of eight metros
  • Basson: the ANC will not control a single metro

Before the vote

On 31 October, Pieter du Toit argued that the municipal election could confirm that the ANC’s decline was speeding up.

After the count

On 5 November he wrote that the ANC was on the brink of falling to second, third or fourth place in seven of the eight metros. The same day, Adriaan Basson noted that the party would not control a single metro.
