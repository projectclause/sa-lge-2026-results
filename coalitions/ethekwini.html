MK’s Mxolisi Phakathi is elected eThekwini mayor with ANC support, and Thabani Luthuli becomes deputy mayor

SABC News, News24, The Witness, Daily Maverick, BusinessTech and others report the MK Party’s Mxolisi Phakathi has been elected eThekwini mayor through a deal with the ANC, ending the ANC’s reign in the metro, and IOL reports MK’s Thabani Luthuli has been elected deputy mayor. The ANC’s Thabani Nyawose was re-elected speaker with MKP support, and Inside Politic reports the DA failed to woo the ANC.
