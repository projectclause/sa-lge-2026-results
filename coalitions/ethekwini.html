eThekwini’s first council sitting ends without a mayor

eThekwini’s inaugural council meeting adjourned on 24 November without electing a mayor, deputy mayor or speaker, to give parties time to finish coalition deals. The sitting went ahead despite a Cogta directive to postpone it, and the ANC and MK would not say whether they had a deal.

Preamble’s AI summary of 24 reports. Updated 24 November 2026.

In brief

  • Several outlets report the council adjourned without filling a single leadership position, to allow coalition talks to conclude.
  • eNCA reports the sitting went ahead despite a directive to postpone it; DailyNews and the Sunday Tribune report the city manager challenged the Cogta directive, and IOL says parties are divided over the postponement.
  • ECR reports smaller parties’ objections were rejected.
  • EWN reports the ANC and MK were coy over whether an agreement had been reached; The National reports the ANC wants two top posts.
  • The MK Party says its mayoral candidate has an “unblemished record”, IOL and The Mercury report.
