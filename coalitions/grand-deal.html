South Africa · National · All eight metros

Should one deal settle all eight metros?

Instead of eight separate fights, Sizwe Mpofu-Walsh floated a single bargain between the DA, ANC and ActionSA over the metro mayors. Others have pitched their own national formulas.

Preamble’s AI summary of 18 reports. Updated 24 November 2026.

In brief

  • Mpofu-Walsh debuted a nationwide coalition idea on 7 November
  • As another commentator describes it, the DA, ANC and ActionSA would share the eight metro mayors
  • Basson expected a DA-ANC deal across the hung councils
  • The DA later proposed that the biggest party leads each hung council

The grand-bargain idea

On his SMWX podcast on 7 November, Mpofu-Walsh set out an idea for one coalition arrangement across the country. A clip of him shared on Facebook is titled how the DA, ANC and ActionSA can divide the eight metros. Commentator Sindile Vabaza describes the split as the DA in Johannesburg, Cape Town, Mangaung and Nelson Mandela Bay, the ANC in Ekurhuleni, eThekwini and Buffalo City, and ActionSA in Tshwane. Preamble has the exact split from Vabaza’s account.

Other national formulas

Adriaan Basson argued on 9 November that a DA-ANC deal modelled on the national unity government was the most likely outcome. On 22 November Geordin Hill-Lewis put a different formula to the ANC and IFP: the party with the most votes in each hung council leads it.

A realignment?

By 13 November Mpofu-Walsh was asking whether South Africa is entering a fundamental political realignment, looking at Mashaba and Moya leading Gauteng metros and an MK-IFP arrangement in KwaZulu-Natal.
