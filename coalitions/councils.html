South Africa · Beyond the metros · Limpopo, Gauteng and KwaZulu-Natal

Outside the metros, councils start to pick partners, and Limpopo’s ANC turns to the DA

With deadlines close, deals are being struck in smaller councils. In Limpopo, the ANC chose the DA over a left-wing bloc, while the IFP expects to seal its KwaZulu-Natal deals this weekend.

Preamble’s AI summary of 8 reports. Updated 24 November 2026.

In brief

  • Sunday World reports the Limpopo ANC chose the DA as its main partner
  • An EFF, SACP and ActionSA bloc failed to take Polokwane
  • Malema proposed the ANC lead 30 of Limpopo’s 39 hung councils and the EFF nine
  • The IFP expects to seal its KwaZulu-Natal deals this weekend
  • Emfuleni’s first sitting collapsed when ANC, EFF, MK and SACP councillors overturned tables and walked out, News24 reports
  • The DA says its coalition holds 47 seats in Emfuleni and called the disruption “political thuggery”

Limpopo

Sunday World reports, citing sources close to the talks, that the ANC chose the DA as its main coalition partner in Limpopo after a bloc of the EFF, SACP and ActionSA failed to take Polokwane. On the EFF’s podcast, Julius Malema had proposed that of the province’s 39 hung councils, the ANC lead 30 and the EFF nine.

KwaZulu-Natal and Gauteng

IFP leader Velenkosini Hlabisa said his party expected to seal its municipal coalition deals this weekend, after meeting the ANC. In Gauteng, SABC News reports Emfuleni’s new council has convened to form a coalition government.
