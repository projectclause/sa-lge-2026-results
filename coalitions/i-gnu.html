What the city deals mean for the unity government

Foreign outlets keep asking whether the local fights will spill into the national unity government. Bloomberg reported that the DA would stay in the GNU for now. Semafor’s Sam Mkokeli called the metros an urban test for it, Justice Malala argued in Bloomberg Opinion that the coalition government has to succeed, and Bloomberg’s Next Africa podcast asked whether it could collapse.

The angle abroad

Outside South Africa, the city talks matter mostly for what they mean for the national government formed in 2024. The question foreign outlets keep asking is whether a DA shut out of the metros will stay in the GNU, and what happens to investor confidence if it does not.

Why it matters to them

For investors and foreign governments, the GNU is the main sign of stability after the ANC lost its majority. That is why Bloomberg treats the DA’s decision to stay as news in itself.
