Geordin Hill-Lewis is re-elected Cape Town mayor and names his mayoral committee

IOL reports Geordin Hill-Lewis has been re-elected mayor of Cape Town, pledging jobs and stronger policing, and that the DA has declared victory in the city. The City of Cape Town, The Bulrushes, EWN, SABC News, Cape Town ETC, Central News South Africa and IOL report he has announced a new mayoral committee, and ActionSA says it will hold the new members accountable.

Preamble’s AI summary of 16 reports. Updated 28 November 2026.

In brief

  • IOL reports Geordin Hill-Lewis was re-elected Cape Town mayor and pledged jobs and stronger policing, and that the DA has declared victory in the Cape Town elections.
  • The City of Cape Town, The Bulrushes, EWN, SABC News, Cape Town ETC and Central News South Africa report Hill-Lewis has announced his new mayoral committee; SABC News and Central News South Africa say it has 11 members, and IOL reports three of them are new.
  • ActionSA says it will hold Cape Town’s newly appointed mayoral committee members accountable for delivering on their mandates.
  • IOL reports Helen Zille defended the DA's privatisation plans, citing Cape Town as an example.
  • Sunday World reports suburban voters secured the DA's election victory in Cape Town.
