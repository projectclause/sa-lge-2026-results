Ekurhuleni’s mayoral election moves to Friday as parties wait on the ANC to sign

Ekurhuleni’s new council held its inaugural meeting on 25 November, but the election of a mayor and speaker was postponed, and SABC News, The National and Inside Metros report the vote will take place on Friday. EWN, The National and Sunday World report parties are waiting on the ANC to sign off the coalition deal, and Newsnote reports Herman Mashaba blamed ANC delays for the stalled talks.

Preamble’s AI summary of 38 reports. Updated 26 November 2026.

In brief

  • The Citizen, EWN, IOL, News24, Briefly News and Business Day report the mayoral election was postponed so coalition partners could finish negotiations; SABC News, The National and Inside Metros report the vote moves to Friday.
  • EWN and The National report parties are waiting on the ANC to sign off the coalition deal, Sunday World reports the ANC’s signature is all that is needed to seal it, and Newsnote reports Mashaba blamed ANC delays for the stalled talks.
  • IOL reports the DA is still pursuing an Ekurhuleni mayoral deal and holds out hope as an ANC-ActionSA deal takes shape.
  • EWN reports the ANC is set to field Mziyanda Mketsu for Ekurhuleni mayor.
  • IOL and the Sunday Independent report Herman Mashaba backed Xolani Khumalo for deputy mayor and warned criminals to 'run'; IOL reports Kagiso Lerutla presided over the inaugural meeting.
