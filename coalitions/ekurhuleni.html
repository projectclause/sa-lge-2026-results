Ekurhuleni’s mayoral election is postponed as coalition talks continue

Ekurhuleni’s new council held its inaugural meeting on 25 November, with IOL reporting Kagiso Lerutla presided, but The Citizen and EWN report the mayoral election was postponed so parties could finish coalition negotiations. IOL reports Herman Mashaba confirmed Xolani Khumalo as Ekurhuleni deputy mayor.

Preamble’s AI summary of 12 reports. Updated 25 November 2026.

In brief

  • The Citizen and EWN report the Ekurhuleni mayoral election and council sitting were postponed for parties to iron out coalition negotiations.
