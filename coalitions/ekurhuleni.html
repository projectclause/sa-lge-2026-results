The ANC's Mziyanda Mketsu is elected Ekurhuleni mayor in a deal with ActionSA and the EFF

The Citizen, IOL and EWN report the ANC's Mziyanda Mketsu has been elected executive mayor of Ekurhuleni, and BusinessTech reports the ANC has taken the metro with ActionSA and the EFF. Inside Politic reports Mketsu has promised a clean administration, and SABC News reports he says residents' interests will be prioritised.

Preamble’s AI summary of 61 reports. Updated 27 November 2026.

In brief

  • The Citizen, IOL and EWN report Mketsu was elected Ekurhuleni mayor after Jacaranda FM reported he was facing off against Rasilingwane for the chain.
  • Inside Politic reports Mketsu promised a clean administration, SABC News reports he says residents' interests will be prioritised, and South African Daily reports he vowed to hit the ground running.
  • BusinessTech reports the ANC has taken Ekurhuleni with ActionSA and the EFF, and IOL reported the MK Party was backing the ANC's candidate after a fallout in Tshwane.
  • IOL, Business Day, EWN and Inside Politic report the EFF's Nthabiseng Tshivhenga was re-elected speaker; IOL says she won 142 votes.
  • The Mercury and other outlets earlier reported the mayoral vote was postponed as the ANC closed in on a deal, and Newsnote reported Mashaba blamed ANC delays.
