Malema takes Zille to court over her Joburg claims

EFF leader Julius Malema went to court to stop DA mayoral candidate Helen Zille repeating claims about him, in a dispute linked to the Johannesburg coalition fight. Zille refused to retract, and the court reserved judgment.

Preamble’s AI summary of 6 reports. Updated 24 November 2026.

In brief

  • Briefly News and the Mail & Guardian report Malema sought an urgent interdict to gag Zille over her Joburg claims.
  • IOL reports Malema demanded a retraction and apology over corruption claims; IOL also reports Zille said no.
  • eNCA describes the case as an alleged defamation battle.
  • IOL reports the court reserved judgment on the bid, which concerns claims of city power capture.
