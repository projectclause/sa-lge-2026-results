Malema awaits judgment in his bid to gag Zille over her Joburg claims

EFF leader Julius Malema went to court to stop DA mayoral candidate Helen Zille repeating claims about him, in a dispute linked to the Johannesburg coalition fight, and the court reserved judgment. Zille refused to retract, and Michael Beaumont has challenged her claims, while Central News South Africa reports Glynnis Breytenbach confirmed recommending Shadrack Sibiya to Herman Mashaba in 2016.

Preamble’s AI summary of 21 reports. Updated 26 November 2026.

In brief

  • Briefly News and the Mail & Guardian report Malema sought an urgent interdict to gag Zille over her Joburg claims.
  • IOL reports Malema demanded a retraction and apology and threatened R1m defamation action; IOL also reports Zille said no, and News24 reports she fought the gag bid using Malema’s own words.
  • IOL reports the court reserved judgment, and eNCA reports Malema is awaiting the outcome.
  • Briefly News reports Michael Beaumont shared a 2016 email that he says contradicts Zille’s claims, and IOL reports Beaumont says the DA recommended Sibiya.
  • Central News South Africa reports Breytenbach confirmed recommending Sibiya to Mashaba in 2016.
