A court bars Zille from repeating her claims about Malema

IOL, News24, SABC News and Central News South Africa report a court has barred DA mayoral candidate Helen Zille from repeating her claims about EFF leader Julius Malema, including that he controlled Herman Mashaba and influenced Shadrack Sibiya's Johannesburg appointment. Sunday World calls it Malema's first-round win in his defamation lawsuit against Zille.

Preamble’s AI summary of 34 reports. Updated 27 November 2026.

In brief

  • News24 reports Zille has been gagged from saying Malema controlled Mashaba, IOL reports the court barred her from repeating corruption allegations against him, and SABC News says the order covers four corruption claims.
  • Central News South Africa reports Zille was ordered to stop alleging Malema influenced Sibiya's Johannesburg appointment.
  • Sunday World reports Malema won the first round of his defamation lawsuit, and newsday.co.za reports he won the case.
  • Malema had sought an urgent interdict and demanded a retraction and apology; IOL reported Zille refused and the court had reserved judgment.
  • Michael Beaumont shared a 2016 email he says contradicts Zille's claims, and Central News South Africa reported Glynnis Breytenbach confirmed recommending Sibiya to Mashaba in 2016.
