The DA is shut out of Theewaterskloof as 16 Western Cape councils stay hung

News24 reports the ANC joined other parties to take control of Theewaterskloof, and the DA warns the new coalition could threaten the municipality’s financial recovery. IOL reports the DA has begun talks as 16 Western Cape councils remain hung, and has named candidates for seven municipalities.
