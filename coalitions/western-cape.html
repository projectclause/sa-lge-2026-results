The DA takes Knysna and Bitou but is shut out of Theewaterskloof, Laingsburg and Langeberg

News24 reports the ANC joined other parties to take control of Theewaterskloof, IOL and the Cape Times report ActionSA formed a Laingsburg coalition with the PA and ANC, and the Cape Argus reports an ANC-PA coalition won Langeberg, all leaving the DA out. The Cape Argus reports the DA has taken Knysna and Bitou, and EWN reports the Knysna deal is with the FF Plus and the Knysna Civic Alliance.

Preamble’s AI summary of 22 reports. Updated 27 November 2026.

In brief

  • News24 reports the ANC and other parties took control of Theewaterskloof, shutting out the DA; the Cape Argus, Cape Times and IOL report the DA warning this could derail financial recovery.
  • IOL and the Cape Times report ActionSA formed a coalition with the PA and ANC in Laingsburg, leaving the DA out, and SABC News reports Matzikama council sits on Thursday after a deadlock over an EFF councillor.
  • EWN reports the DA secured Knysna through a coalition agreement with the FF Plus and the Knysna Civic Alliance; the Cape Argus reports the DA has taken Knysna and Bitou, while an ANC-PA coalition won Langeberg.
  • News24 reports the DA and FF Plus entered coalitions in some hung councils; IOL reported the DA began talks with 16 councils hung.
  • IOL and the Weekend Argus carry an argument that the opposition’s voice in Cape Town is growing smaller and meeker.
