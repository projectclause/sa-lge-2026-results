The DA is shut out of Theewaterskloof and Laingsburg as Western Cape councils pick partners

News24 reports the ANC joined other parties to take control of Theewaterskloof, and IOL reports ActionSA formed a Laingsburg coalition with the PA and ANC, leaving the DA out. News24 reports the DA and FF Plus have entered coalitions in some hung Western Cape councils.

Preamble’s AI summary of 12 reports. Updated 25 November 2026.

In brief

  • News24 reports the ANC and other parties took control of Theewaterskloof, shutting out the DA; the Cape Argus and IOL report the DA warning this could threaten financial recovery.
  • IOL reports ActionSA formed a coalition with the PA and ANC in Laingsburg, leaving the DA out.
  • News24 reports the DA and FF Plus entered coalitions in some hung councils; IOL reported the DA began talks with 16 councils hung.
  • IOL reports the DA named deputy mayor and speaker candidates for seven Western Cape municipalities, and Limberg as Cape Town deputy mayor.
  • IOL and the Weekend Argus carry an argument that the opposition’s voice in Cape Town is growing smaller and meeker.
