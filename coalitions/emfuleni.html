The DA secures a five-party coalition in Emfuleni after a sitting collapses

ANC, EFF and MK councillors disrupted Emfuleni’s mayoral election sitting, and Nova News reports it collapsed. News24 reports the DA has secured a five-party coalition to take control of the municipality, and the DA now accuses the Gauteng government of wilful delays.

Preamble’s AI summary of 10 reports. Updated 26 November 2026.

In brief

  • News24 reports chaos as ANC, EFF and MK councillors disrupted the Emfuleni mayoral election sitting, Nova News reports the sitting collapsed, and The South African Star reports police were called in despite a DA warning.
  • The DA’s Ashor Sarupen condemned what he called EFF violence at the Emfuleni council sitting, in a statement carried by Politicsweb.
  • News24 reports the DA secured a five-party coalition to take control of Emfuleni, giving up MMC posts, the speaker and the chief whip.
  • The DA says, in a statement carried by Politicsweb, that the Gauteng government’s wilful delays in Emfuleni will not delay democracy, and eNCA reports Emfuleni faces a crucial leadership vote.
