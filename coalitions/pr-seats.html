The ANC turns down ActionSA’s offer of council seats

The ANC won no PR seats in Gauteng’s three metros, which could keep some of its regional leaders out of council. ActionSA offered to bring ANC members in through its own lists. A senior Gauteng ANC source told The Citizen the answer is no.

In brief

  • The ANC won no PR seats in Johannesburg, Tshwane or Ekurhuleni
  • Regional leaders like Loyiso Masuku could be left out of council for five years
  • ActionSA offered some of its PR seats, using dual membership

  • ANC regional secretary Sasabona Manganye confirmed the party turned the offer down, the Mail & Guardian reports

  • Manganye told IOL taking an ActionSA seat would be “madness” and against the ANC’s constitution

Why the ANC has no PR seats

The ANC won more wards than any other party in the three metros, so its ward seats already exceeded its share of the vote and it was given no PR seats. EWN reports that its regional leaders who stood as PR candidates, including Johannesburg chair Loyiso Masuku, Tshwane chair Eugene Modise and Ekurhuleni leader Doctor Xhakaza, could miss out.

The offer

Michael Beaumont told EWN that ActionSA would be amenable to one ANC person per municipality coming through an ActionSA PR seat, using a dual-membership provision in its constitution.

Reported: the ANC says no

A senior Gauteng ANC source told The Citizen the provincial task team rejected the offer, saying an ANC member who takes it up must choose between the ANC and ActionSA. The SACP said its seats are not open to any party, and that lists already gazetted cannot be changed.

Why ActionSA offered

Michael Beaumont told the Mail & Guardian ActionSA was willing to accommodate Masuku because the ANC had made significant concessions in the negotiations. “It is for them to say whether they pursue it or not,” he said.
