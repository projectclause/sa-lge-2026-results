The ANC leaves coalition choices to its provinces and regions

EWN and Dan Corder report the ANC’s NEC has let provinces and regions decide coalitions, with consequences for Gauteng, eThekwini, Nelson Mandela Bay and over 100 hung councils. The ANC says there is consensus as talks move towards conclusion, while TimesLIVE says Ramaphosa faces a unity test.

Preamble’s AI summary of 7 reports. Updated 24 November 2026.

In brief

  • EWN asks whether the ANC has given provinces and regions the final say; Dan Corder says the NEC decided to let regional bodies choose, affecting well over 100 hung councils.
  • Newsnote reports the ANC asserting there is consensus as talks near conclusion; The Witness reports the NEC weighing up its coalition future.
  • Business Day reports the ANC is seeking a foothold in hung councils, and EWN quotes an analyst saying its choices could decide whether it enters a 'death spiral'.
  • On Gabriel Makin’s channel, Marius Roodt argues the choice matters less than people think, noting the Gauteng ANC wants to work with MK and the EFF.
  • TimesLIVE reports Ramaphosa faces a tough ANC unity test, and The Common Sense argues ANC indecision has left a power vacuum.
