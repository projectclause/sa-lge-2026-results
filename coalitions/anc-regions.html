The ANC leaves coalition choices to its provinces and regions

EWN and Dan Corder report the ANC’s NEC has let provinces and regions decide coalitions, affecting over 100 hung councils, and SABC News reports the NEC resolved that DA coalitions should be a last resort. IOL and The Common Sense report the ANC has moved Fikile Mbalula’s coalition briefing to Friday, ahead of crucial Gauteng mayoral votes.
