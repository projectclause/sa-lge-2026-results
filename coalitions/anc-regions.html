The ANC leaves coalition choices to its provinces and regions and sets partners a 100-day clock

EWN and Dan Corder report the ANC’s NEC has let provinces and regions decide coalitions, affecting over 100 hung councils, and SABC News and IOL report the NEC resolved that DA coalitions should be a last resort. Polity.org.za reports the ANC has unveiled a coalition co-operation accord to stabilise 139 hung councils, and News24 reports the public accord puts coalition partners on a 100-day clock.

Preamble’s AI summary of 57 reports. Updated 28 November 2026.

In brief

  • EWN asks whether the ANC has given provinces and regions the final say; Dan Corder says the NEC let regional bodies choose, affecting well over 100 hung councils, and SABC News and IOL report the NEC resolved that the DA should be a 'last resort'.
  • Polity.org.za reports the ANC has unveiled a coalition co-operation accord to stabilise 139 hung councils, and News24 reports the public accord puts coalition partners on a 100-day clock; EWN quotes an analyst warning of a 'death spiral'.
  • The Citizen reports Mbalula said 'the ANC did not win this election' as the party sealed coalition agreements.
  • IOL reports Mkhize warned the ANC against a DA coalition in a 2024 letter, and that the SACP drew a line over coalition support; The Common Sense reports the SACP called a Sol Plaatje deal with the DA a “betrayal”.
  • TimesLIVE and Business Day report Ramaphosa says he is 'not resigning' after the election setback, Briefly News reports him dismissing resignation rumours, and SABC News reports he urged ANC members to raise concerns within party structures.
