Abroad, Johannesburg is the story: a deal to shut out the DA

Johannesburg gets the most attention outside South Africa. Reuters and the FT reported that the ANC and its partners were close to a deal that would sideline the DA, which won the most votes in the city. The FT reported that Helen Zille had told allies her bid for mayor was over, and The Africa Report called Herman Mashaba the kingmaker in Gauteng.

Preamble’s AI summary of 6 reports. Updated 20 November 2026.

In brief

  • Reuters reported on 19 November that the ANC and its partners were near a deal that would sideline the DA in Johannesburg, though the DA topped the poll there
  • The FT had reported the ANC nearing a coalition deal in the city the day before
  • The FT then reported that Helen Zille, whom it calls South Africa’s “Iron Lady”, had told allies her mayoral bid was over
  • The Africa Report named Herman Mashaba the kingmaker in Gauteng
  • For foreign readers, the story is that the party with the most votes may not govern Africa’s richest city

The angle abroad

Foreign outlets tell the Johannesburg story through the paradox at its centre: the DA won the most votes but may be shut out of power. Reuters puts that paradox in its headline.

What they leave out

Judging by their headlines, foreign outlets give less room than South African ones to the share-out of mayoral posts or the timing of council sittings. They focus on who ends up in charge and what that says about the ANC.
