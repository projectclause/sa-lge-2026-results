Faku returns in Buffalo City with the EFF as a DA-led coalition signs for Nelson Mandela Bay

Princess Faku was re-elected Buffalo City mayor, beating the UDM’s Nqabayomzi Kwankwa, and BusinessTech reports the ANC teamed up with the EFF. In Nelson Mandela Bay, six parties signed a DA-led coalition deal, but News24 reports an ANC-led filibuster blocked the vote for mayor and speaker.

Preamble’s AI summary of 11 reports. Updated 24 November 2026.

In brief

  • eNCA, EWN and Briefly News report Faku was re-elected; Briefly News says she defeated the UDM’s Nqabayomzi Kwankwa, and BusinessTech says the ANC is running the metro with the EFF.
  • News24 reports ANC divisions over Faku’s return, quoting one voice saying “you cannot smuggle her back”.
  • News24 says the DA will govern Nelson Mandela Bay with the FF Plus, UDM, ActionSA and PAC; several outlets report a six-party deal with Retief Odendaal as mayor.
  • News24 reports the DA’s plan to elect a mayor and speaker was blocked by an ANC-led filibuster, and Daily Maverick says the ANC scrambled for a last-minute coalition.
