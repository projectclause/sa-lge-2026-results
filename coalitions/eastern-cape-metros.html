Faku returns in Buffalo City with the EFF as the DA's Retief Odendaal returns as Nelson Mandela Bay mayor

Princess Faku was re-elected Buffalo City mayor, beating the UDM's Nqabayomzi Kwankwa, and BusinessTech and The South African report the ANC teamed up with the EFF. In Nelson Mandela Bay, IOL, EWN, News24, Daily Maverick and Algoa FM report the DA's Retief Odendaal has reclaimed the mayoralty in a multiparty coalition deal after an earlier ANC-led filibuster blocked the vote.

Preamble’s AI summary of 52 reports. Updated 27 November 2026.

In brief

  • eNCA, EWN and Briefly News report Faku was re-elected; BusinessTech and The South African say the ANC joined forces with the EFF to keep control of the metro.
  • News24 reports ANC divisions over Faku's return, quoting one voice saying “you cannot smuggle her back”.
  • IOL, EWN, Daily Maverick and Algoa FM report Odendaal was elected Nelson Mandela Bay mayor, and News24 reports the council elected new leadership as he took the helm.
  • News24 says the DA will govern with the FF Plus, UDM, ActionSA and PAC, after earlier reporting the vote was blocked by an ANC-led filibuster.
  • Geordin Hill-Lewis welcomed Odendaal's election, and the DA says its new mayor brings new hope and stability.
