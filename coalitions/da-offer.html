South Africa · National · Gauteng metros and 137 hung councils

The DA offers the ANC 93 mayoralties; ActionSA rejects its Gauteng deal

The DA’s nationwide framework would have the ANC lead 93 hung councils, the DA 24 and the IFP 20. A separate Gauteng offer, which ActionSA rejected, would have let ActionSA run Tshwane while the DA led Johannesburg and Ekurhuleni.

Preamble’s AI summary of 52 reports. Updated 25 November 2026.

In brief

  • The DA proposes the ANC lead 93 of 137 hung councils, the DA 24 and the IFP 20
  • The DA would take Johannesburg, Tshwane, Ekurhuleni, eThekwini and Nelson Mandela Bay
  • No mayoralties go to MK or the PA
  • The DA says the ANC has not yet replied
  • ActionSA rejected a separate DA offer for the Gauteng metros
  • On 702, Beaumont called that offer “a smash and grab”

The numbers

Daily Maverick reports that the DA’s “stability framework”, sent to ANC secretary-general Fikile Mbalula on 20 November and signed by Ashor Sarupen, gives the ANC 93 mayoralties, the DA 24 and the IFP 20, subject to the IFP agreeing. The largest party among the partners in each council would lead it. Nineteen councils where the ANC and SACP already have a majority would be left out.

Gauteng: an offer ActionSA would not take

Under the DA’s separate Gauteng offer, Nasiphi Moya would stay mayor of Tshwane while the DA led Johannesburg and Ekurhuleni, as a confidence-and-supply arrangement. ActionSA said it would have had to back DA governments in two metros without seats on their mayoral committees, and would not support a Helen Zille mayoralty.

On 702

Geordin Hill-Lewis told Clement Manyathela that “the power of the deal is in its simplicity”, that the Gauteng metros are the biggest obstacle, and that he had spoken to Ramaphosa the night before. Michael Beaumont said the DA had not met ActionSA until last Thursday and called its offer “a smash and grab”.
