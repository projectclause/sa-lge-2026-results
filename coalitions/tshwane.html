ActionSA’s condition for any deal: Moya stays in Tshwane

ActionSA made keeping Nasiphi Moya as Tshwane mayor a condition for any coalition, and News24’s Adriaan Basson expected her back.

Preamble’s AI summary of 25 reports. Updated 26 November 2026.

In brief

  • Mashaba: any deal must keep Moya as mayor
  • Basson expected Moya could return in Tshwane
  • Beaumont: ActionSA is set to lead Tshwane again
  • The new council sits for the first time on Thursday
  • Sussman says the ANC appears committed to a four-party deal with ActionSA, the EFF and MK

A condition set on election night

Speaking to Clement Manyathela at the IEC results centre, Herman Mashaba said any coalition involving ActionSA must keep Nasiphi Moya as Tshwane mayor, and that ActionSA could set terms with both the DA and the ANC.

Expected back

On 9 November, Adriaan Basson wrote that Moya could return as mayor under a broad DA-ANC arrangement. On 22 November Michael Beaumont said ActionSA was set to lead Tshwane again.
