Editorial standards

Preamble follows the principles of the Press Code of South Africa: we report accurately, credit our sources, separate news from opinion, give people the chance to respond, and correct our mistakes. Preamble is not a member of the Press Council.

Last updated 27 November 2026.

What we publish

Preamble tracks South African politics with data: election results, the coalition talks in the metros, the mayors they elect, the Madlanga Commission and South Africans abroad. Most of what we show is drawn from official results and from reporting by established news organisations, which we credit and link to.

Accuracy and attribution

We report facts we can check, and we say where they come from. Every story on the Coalitions Tracker and every line on the Madlanga Commission tracker links to the original report. Where we summarise someone else’s reporting, we name the outlet. We do not present allegations as facts: we say who made them.

News and opinion

Commentary is labelled as such. On our trackers, analysis and opinion sit in their own “Voices” sections, separate from the reporting.

The right to respond

When people are named in serious allegations, we include their published response where there is one. Being named in evidence or in a news report is not a finding of wrongdoing, and a criminal charge is not a conviction; we say so where it matters.

Children, victims and privacy

We do not identify children involved in crime, or victims of sexual offences. We do not identify witnesses that a court or commission has ordered protected, and we do not report evidence heard in private. We treat the families of victims with care.

How automation and AI are used

Parts of this site update themselves every hour. Software collects new reports from established news organisations and adds them to our trackers. Some summaries are drafted with the help of AI software from those reports, and they are always credited to the reports they come from.

On the Madlanga Commission tracker, automatic lines about people are only ever a news outlet’s own headline, quoted word for word and credited. Rules decide what is shown: only established newsrooms count, headlines that state guilt or involve children or sexual offences are held back, and charges or changes to a witness’s status appear only once two outlets report them. Every automatic decision is logged.

Corrections

If we get something wrong, we fix it as quickly as we can and, where the mistake mattered, we say what we changed. To report an error, email preamblehq@gmail.com with the page and what is wrong.
