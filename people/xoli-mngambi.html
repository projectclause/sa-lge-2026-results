Who is Xoli Mngambi?

Xoli Mngambi (Anchor, Newzroom Afrika) is one of the journalists and commentators Preamble’s Coalitions Tracker follows on the coalition talks after South Africa’s 4 November 2026 local government elections.

What is Xoli Mngambi’s latest on the coalitions?

On 24 November 2026, Newzroom Afrika: MK Party’s busy with multi-party coalition negotiations in eThekwini. Mngambi spoke to MK’s KwaZulu-Natal leader Willies Mchunu, who said the national leadership was leading talks with the ANC, EFF, ActionSA and IFP, and that the DA had approached the party, though he knew of no concrete engagement.
