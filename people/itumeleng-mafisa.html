Who is Itumeleng Mafisa?

Itumeleng Mafisa (Journalist, The Citizen) is one of the journalists and commentators Preamble’s Coalitions Tracker follows on the coalition talks after South Africa’s 4 November 2026 local government elections.

What has Itumeleng Mafisa said about the coalition talks?

  • Reported Mashaba’s condition that Zille and Brink step aside as mayoral candidates. Story: Joburg will elect its mayor on Friday as parties finish their deal
  • Followed the PR-seat offer for The Citizen, from Mashaba’s pitch to the ANC’s rejection. Story: The ANC turns down ActionSA’s offer of council seats
  • Reported the ANC rejecting claims that the coalition talks drove the rand down. Story: Will the coalition fights break the national unity government?

What is Itumeleng Mafisa’s latest on the coalitions?

On 23 November 2026, The Citizen: ANC rejects Mashaba’s PR seat horse-trading offer. Mafisa reported that, according to a senior Gauteng ANC source, the provincial task team rejected Mashaba’s offer, saying an ANC member who takes it up must choose between the ANC and ActionSA. The SACP said its seats are not available.
