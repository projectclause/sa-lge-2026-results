Who is Penuel Mlotshwa?

Penuel Mlotshwa (Podcaster and commentator, “The Black Pen”) is one of the journalists and commentators Preamble’s Coalitions Tracker follows on the coalition talks after South Africa’s 4 November 2026 local government elections.

What has Penuel Mlotshwa said about the coalition talks?

  • Backed ActionSA in Johannesburg, distrusts its voters who want a DA deal, warns the DA it won’t win by being arrogant, and wants coalition talks filmed live. Story: Joburg will elect its mayor on Friday as parties finish their deal
  • Warned that the DA won’t win the coalition games if it is arrogant and condescending. Story: The DA offers the ANC 93 mayoralties; ActionSA rejects its Gauteng deal
  • Wants coalition negotiations filmed live and made public, not held in secret. Story: Should one deal settle all eight metros?

What is Penuel Mlotshwa’s latest on the coalitions?

On 15 November 2026, X: Coalition negotiations should be filmed live. Mlotshwa said coalition negotiations should be filmed live and made public rather than held in secret, and followed it with a video making the same case.
