Who is Herman Mashaba?

Herman Mashaba (ActionSA leader) is one of the key players in the coalition talks after South Africa’s 4 November 2026 local government elections.

What has Herman Mashaba said about the coalition talks?

  • Would not work with Zille or Brink, says the DA tried to buy his support, and is set to be Joburg mayor in a deal with the ANC. Story: Joburg will elect its mayor on Friday as parties finish their deal
  • Said the Johannesburg council meeting would go ahead and the partners would make a final announcement this week. Story: The ANC turns down ActionSA’s offer of council seats
  • Says the DA’s refusal to drop Zille and Brink stalled the talks, that coalition talks need not be a contest, and has defended ActionSA’s choices. Story: ActionSA bargains with both big parties, then leans towards the ANC
  • Made keeping Nasiphi Moya as Tshwane mayor a condition of any deal, and named the DA’s Cilliers Brink as a reason talks stalled. Story: ActionSA’s condition for any deal: Moya stays in Tshwane
