Who is Herman Mashaba?

Herman Mashaba (ActionSA leader, mayor of Johannesburg) is one of the key players in the coalition talks after South Africa’s 4 November 2026 local government elections.

What has Herman Mashaba said about the coalition talks?

  • Would not work with Zille or Brink, says the DA tried to buy his support, and is set to be Joburg mayor in a deal with the ANC. Story: Joburg council meets to elect a mayor as reports say MK is out of the deal
  • Said the Johannesburg council meeting would go ahead and the partners would make a final announcement this week. Story: The ANC turns down ActionSA’s offer of council seats
  • Says the DA’s refusal to drop Zille and Brink stalled the talks, that coalition talks need not be a contest, and has defended ActionSA’s choices. Story: ActionSA bargains with both big parties, then leans towards the ANC
  • Made keeping Nasiphi Moya as Tshwane mayor a condition of any deal, and named the DA’s Cilliers Brink as a reason talks stalled. Story: ActionSA’s condition for any deal: Moya stays in Tshwane

What is Herman Mashaba’s latest on the coalitions?

On 9 November 2026, EWN: On 702: any deal must keep Moya as Tshwane mayor. Speaking to Clement Manyathela on election night, Mashaba said ActionSA could set terms with both the DA and the ANC, that Nasiphi Moya must stay as Tshwane mayor, and that he would not negotiate with a DA team that includes Helen Zille.
