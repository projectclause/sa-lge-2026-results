Who is Prince Mashele?

Prince Mashele (Political analyst and author) is one of the journalists and commentators Preamble’s Coalitions Tracker follows on the coalition talks after South Africa’s 4 November 2026 local government elections.

What has Prince Mashele said about the coalition talks?

  • Argues an ActionSA–DA coalition is the only viable way forward, and says both parties asked him to help bring them together. Story: Joburg will elect its mayor on Friday as parties finish their deal
  • On The Hustlers Corner SA, questioned the ANC’s coalition deals. Story: Should one deal settle all eight metros?
  • Wants ActionSA to govern the Gauteng metros with the DA, not the ANC. Story: ActionSA bargains with both big parties, then leans towards the ANC
  • Predicts the ANC could fall below 15% by 2029, blaming corruption, incompetence and arrogance. Story: The ANC loses its grip on South Africa’s metros
