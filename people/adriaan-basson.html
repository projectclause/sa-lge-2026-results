Who is Adriaan Basson?

Adriaan Basson (Journalist, News24) is one of the journalists and commentators Preamble’s Coalitions Tracker follows on the coalition talks after South Africa’s 4 November 2026 local government elections.

What has Adriaan Basson said about the coalition talks?

  • Reports that Zille has conceded, leaving the DA in opposition despite topping the vote. Story: Joburg council meets to elect a mayor as reports say MK is out of the deal
  • Expected a DA-ANC deal modelled on the national unity government across the hung councils. Story: Should one deal settle all eight metros?
  • Expects Moya could return as mayor under a broad DA-ANC deal. Story: ActionSA’s condition for any deal: Moya stays in Tshwane
  • Named four coalition scenarios (Ukraine, Lesotho, Rainbow and Ghana), expected a DA-ANC deal, then warned the fight over the cities could bring down the unity government. Story: Will the coalition fights break the national unity government?
  • Noted that the ANC will not control a single metro. Story: The ANC loses its grip on South Africa’s metros
