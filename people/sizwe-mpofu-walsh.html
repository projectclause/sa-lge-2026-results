Who is Sizwe Mpofu-Walsh?

Sizwe Mpofu-Walsh (Political commentator, host of SMWX) is one of the journalists and commentators Preamble’s Coalitions Tracker follows on the coalition talks after South Africa’s 4 November 2026 local government elections.

What has Sizwe Mpofu-Walsh said about the coalition talks?

  • Floated one bargain between the DA, ANC and ActionSA to share out the metro mayors, and asks whether South Africa is entering a political realignment. Story: Should one deal settle all eight metros?
  • Asked whether the DA’s vote share entitles it to govern the metros, as talks reached crunch time. Story: ActionSA bargains with both big parties, then leans towards the ANC
  • Asks whether the unity government survives the local results, and has set out four possible coalition agreements. Story: Will the coalition fights break the national unity government?
