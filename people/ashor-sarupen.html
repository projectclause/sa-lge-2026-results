Who is Ashor Sarupen?

Ashor Sarupen (DA Federal Council chairperson) is one of the key players in the coalition talks after South Africa’s 4 November 2026 local government elections.

What has Ashor Sarupen said about the coalition talks?

  • Says the DA has not been shut out and that talks with all parties are continuing. Story: Joburg council meets to elect a mayor as reports say MK is out of the deal
  • Signed the DA’s letter to Mbalula proposing that the DA, ANC and IFP co-govern the hung councils. Story: The DA offers the ANC 93 mayoralties; ActionSA rejects its Gauteng deal

What is Ashor Sarupen’s latest on the coalitions?

On 21 November 2026, News24: Sarupen: talks with all parties are continuing. As News24 reported the ANC drawing MK into a deal with ActionSA, Sarupen disputed that the DA had been shut out, saying its talks with all parties were continuing.
