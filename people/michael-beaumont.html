Who is Michael Beaumont?

Michael Beaumont (ActionSA national chairperson) is one of the key players in the coalition talks after South Africa’s 4 November 2026 local government elections.

What has Michael Beaumont said about the coalition talks?

  • Says a deal with the ANC, EFF and MK is 90 to 95% agreed, with Mashaba as mayor and the decision due on Sunday evening. On 23 November he told EWN the Johannesburg council would most likely be adjourned to Wednesday or so to elect a mayor. Story: Joburg council meets to elect a mayor as reports say MK is out of the deal
  • Said ActionSA offered a PR seat because the ANC had made significant concessions, and left it to the ANC to decide. Story: The ANC turns down ActionSA’s offer of council seats
  • Rejected the DA’s Gauteng offer and told 702 it was “a smash and grab”, not a negotiation. Story: The DA offers the ANC 93 mayoralties; ActionSA rejects its Gauteng deal
  • Says coalitions, not vote share, decide who governs, and that ActionSA serves at the pleasure of no other party. Story: ActionSA bargains with both big parties, then leans towards the ANC
  • Says ActionSA is set to lead Tshwane again. Story: ActionSA’s condition for any deal: Moya stays in Tshwane

What is Michael Beaumont’s latest on the coalitions?

On 22 November 2026, The National: ActionSA snubs DA as ANC weighs coalition deals with MK Party. Beaumont pushed back at critics, mostly DA supporters, saying ActionSA does not serve at the pleasure of any other party. He said it is set to lead Tshwane again and take over in Johannesburg.
