Who is Bongani Baloyi?

Bongani Baloyi (MK, first on its Johannesburg list) is one of the key players in the coalition talks after South Africa’s 4 November 2026 local government elections.

What has Bongani Baloyi said about the coalition talks?

  • Was tipped in reports as Mashaba’s deputy mayor; the post went to the ANC’s Thapelo Radebe. Story: Mashaba is elected Joburg mayor in an ANC-backed deal, leaving the top-polling DA in opposition
  • Tipped, in reports, as Mashaba’s deputy mayor in Johannesburg. Story: MK talked to everyone, the DA included, then took eThekwini with the ANC

What is Bongani Baloyi’s latest on the coalitions?

On 22 November 2026, Sunday World: ActionSA open to Bongani Baloyi as Mashaba’s deputy mayor in Joburg power deal. Sunday World reported that MK’s Baloyi, a former DA mayor of Midvaal who left ActionSA in 2023, is in the running to be Mashaba’s deputy in Johannesburg, and that ActionSA is open to it, according to people familiar with the talks. It depends on what the ANC and EFF demand.
