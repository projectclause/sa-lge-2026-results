Who is Kamogelo Moichela?

Kamogelo Moichela (Journalist, IOL) is one of the journalists and commentators Preamble’s Coalitions Tracker follows on the coalition talks after South Africa’s 4 November 2026 local government elections.

What has Kamogelo Moichela said about the coalition talks?

  • Followed the Joburg deal for IOL, from smaller parties backing Mashaba to the DA’s legal letter and MK’s Baloyi as deputy. Story: Joburg council meets to elect a mayor as reports say MK is out of the deal
  • Reported the ANC’s offer of the Joburg and Tshwane mayoralties to ActionSA. Story: ActionSA bargains with both big parties, then leans towards the ANC

What is Kamogelo Moichela’s latest on the coalitions?

On 23 November 2026, IOL: DA threatens legal action over Johannesburg council’s inaugural meeting. Moichela reported that the DA served city manager Floyd Brink a lawyer’s letter asking for the first meeting to be postponed, arguing the legal deadline was 27 November. He reported that MK’s Bongani Baloyi was expected to take the deputy mayor post.
