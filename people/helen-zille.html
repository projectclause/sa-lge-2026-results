Who is Helen Zille?

Helen Zille (DA mayoral candidate, Johannesburg) is one of the key players in the coalition talks after South Africa’s 4 November 2026 local government elections.

What has Helen Zille said about the coalition talks?

  • Said the DA would not go to court to delay the council, had made its final offer and would fight for Joburg from the opposition if needed. Story: Joburg will elect its mayor on Friday as parties finish their deal
  • Before the vote, said coalitions are often about choosing the least bad option. Story: Should one deal settle all eight metros?

What is Helen Zille’s latest on the coalitions?

On 23 November 2026, News24: Zille calls for electoral reform to stop small parties ‘making government unstable’. Speaking to News24 before Johannesburg’s first sitting, Zille said the DA had submitted a private member’s bill for a 2% vote threshold for any party to be represented in legislatures and councils, calling it undemocratic that parties with under 1% can shape who governs. The DA has 91 of Johannesburg’s 270 seats.
