Who is Darren Campher?

Darren Campher (Political commentator) is one of the journalists and commentators Preamble’s Coalitions Tracker follows on the coalition talks after South Africa’s 4 November 2026 local government elections.

What is Darren Campher’s latest on the coalitions?

On 23 November 2026, TikTok: “Are you ready for the doomsday coalition?”. Campher shared an ANC leader saying the door was closed on the DA and that the party’s proposed Johannesburg government left out the DA, FF Plus and ACDP, then predicted a media storm over the ANC, ActionSA, EFF and MK coalition and said it must now perform.
