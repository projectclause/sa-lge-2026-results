Who is Paul Berkowitz?

Paul Berkowitz (Political analyst, Daily Maverick) is one of the journalists and commentators Preamble’s Coalitions Tracker follows on the coalition talks after South Africa’s 4 November 2026 local government elections.

What has Paul Berkowitz said about the coalition talks?

  • Before the vote, found MK first in all four eThekwini polls, with the opposition reaching a majority in only two scenarios. Story: MK talked to everyone, the DA included, then took eThekwini with the ANC
  • Argues an ANC–ActionSA pact is likelier than a DA deal in Gauteng, because more parties are willing to join it. Story: ActionSA bargained with both big parties, chose the ANC, and now runs Joburg and Tshwane
  • Says the ANC is “bleeding from a thousand cuts” to ActionSA, the DA, the UDM, the SACP and local movements. Story: The ANC loses its grip on the metros, but keeps two mayors through coalition deals

What is Paul Berkowitz’s latest on the coalitions?

On 17 November 2026, Daily Maverick: When the horse trading is over, what coalitions will govern Gauteng?. Berkowitz argues two of the DA, ANC and ActionSA will end up running Gauteng, and that an ANC–ActionSA pact looks likelier: it needs more partners, but more parties, including the EFF, SACP and MK, are willing to join it. ActionSA is the kingmaker in Ekurhuleni.
