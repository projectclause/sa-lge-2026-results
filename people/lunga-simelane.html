Who is Lunga Simelane?

Lunga Simelane (Political journalist, Daily Maverick) is one of the journalists and commentators Preamble’s Coalitions Tracker follows on the coalition talks after South Africa’s 4 November 2026 local government elections.

What has Lunga Simelane said about the coalition talks?

  • Reported that the FF Plus tried and failed to broker a DA–ActionSA deal between Zille and Mashaba. Story: Joburg council meets to elect a mayor as reports say MK is out of the deal
  • Reported the DA’s written “stability framework”, sent to Mbalula, and ActionSA’s rejection of its Gauteng offer. Story: The DA offers the ANC 93 mayoralties; ActionSA rejects its Gauteng deal
  • Reported that ActionSA’s support would likely decide who governs Gauteng’s three metros. Story: ActionSA bargains with both big parties, then leans towards the ANC
  • Reported that the ANC had lost the lead in four metros and must choose between coalition deals and opposition. Story: The ANC loses its grip on South Africa’s metros

What is Lunga Simelane’s latest on the coalitions?

On 7 November 2026, Daily Maverick: ANC faces coalition scramble as control of key metros slips away. Simelane reported that, with councils due to sit within 14 days, the ANC had lost the lead in Johannesburg, Tshwane and Ekurhuleni to the DA and in eThekwini to MK, leaving it to choose between coalition deals and opposition.
