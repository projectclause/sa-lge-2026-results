Who is Tessa Dooms?

Tessa Dooms (Political analyst, director of the Rivonia Circle) is one of the journalists and commentators Preamble’s Coalitions Tracker follows on the coalition talks after South Africa’s 4 November 2026 local government elections.

What has Tessa Dooms said about the coalition talks?

  • Called the DA’s outreach to MK one of the surprises of the talks, and said MK had learnt to respect its voters’ wishes. Story: MK talks to everyone, the DA included, but says it wants like-minded partners
  • Said the ANC’s dramatic decline is the defining feature of the results, reflecting disillusionment with incumbents rather than enthusiasm for any one party. Story: The ANC loses its grip on South Africa’s metros

What is Tessa Dooms’s latest on the coalitions?

On 17 November 2026, Kaya 959: Why Herman Mashaba holds the cards in DA coalition talks. Dooms told Kaya 959 that the ANC appears to see a deal with the DA as a risk because of how its own supporters feel about it, and that the 14-day window may not prevent a revolving door of mayors. She called the DA’s outreach to MK, a party it had described as unconstitutional, one of the surprises of the talks, and said MK had learnt to respect its voters’ wishes.
