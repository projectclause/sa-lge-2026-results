Who is Thabiso Goba?

Thabiso Goba (Reporter, EWN) is one of the journalists and commentators Preamble’s Coalitions Tracker follows on the coalition talks after South Africa’s 4 November 2026 local government elections.

What has Thabiso Goba said about the coalition talks?

  • Reported for EWN how the ANC’s lack of PR seats could keep its regional leaders out of the Gauteng councils. Story: The ANC turns down ActionSA’s offer of council seats
  • Reported both DA offers for EWN: the 93-24-20 split of hung councils, and ActionSA’s rejection of the Gauteng deal. Story: The DA offers the ANC 93 mayoralties; ActionSA rejects its Gauteng deal
  • Reported that eThekwini would sit on Tuesday despite Cogta’s order to postpone. Story: MK talked to everyone, the DA included, then took eThekwini with the ANC
  • Reported that ActionSA’s objection is to Zille and Brink, not the DA, and that it will back the DA in Nelson Mandela Bay. Story: ActionSA bargained with both big parties, chose the ANC, and now runs Joburg and Tshwane

What is Thabiso Goba’s latest on the coalitions?

On 22 November 2026, EWN: DA makes offer to ANC, IFP to co-govern 137 hung municipalities across SA. Goba reported the DA’s proposal that the party with the most votes in each hung council should lead it, giving the ANC 93 mayors, the DA 24 and the IFP 20. Neither the ANC nor the IFP had accepted it yet.
