Who is Gabriel Makin?

Gabriel Makin (Research director, Social Research Foundation; host, Conversations with Gabriel) is one of the journalists and commentators Preamble’s Coalitions Tracker follows on the coalition talks after South Africa’s 4 November 2026 local government elections.

  • Discussed with Marius Roodt why a deal with MK and the EFF may suit the ANC. Story: Should one deal settle all eight metros?
  • Interviewed Corné Mulder, who said ActionSA was wrong to negotiate with the ANC after voters rejected it. Story: ActionSA bargains with both big parties, then leans towards the ANC
  • Interviewed Tony Leon on why the ANC still holds most of the cards despite losing the metros. Story: The ANC loses its grip on South Africa’s metros
