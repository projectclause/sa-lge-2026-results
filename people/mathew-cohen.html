Who is Mathew Cohen?

Mathew Cohen (Host, The Power Pod) is one of the journalists and commentators Preamble’s Coalitions Tracker follows on the coalition talks after South Africa’s 4 November 2026 local government elections.

What has Mathew Cohen said about the coalition talks?

  • Went through the DA’s final offer to ActionSA and ActionSA’s response on social media. Story: The DA offers the ANC 93 mayoralties; ActionSA rejects its Gauteng deal
  • Covered the DA and ANC entering a coalition in Limpopo. Story: Outside the metros, councils start to pick partners, and Limpopo’s ANC turns to the DA
  • Did the coalition maths on the metros, and covered ActionSA asking the FF Plus to mediate its talks with the DA. Story: ActionSA bargains with both big parties, then leans towards the ANC

What is Mathew Cohen’s latest on the coalitions?

On 9 November 2026, The Power Pod: South Africa’s new coalitions may have just been built by one man. Cohen asked whether party leaders hold real power in the coalition talks, or whether one wealthy backer could decide South Africa’s political future.
