Who is Geordin Hill-Lewis?

Geordin Hill-Lewis (DA leader) is one of the key players in the coalition talks after South Africa’s 4 November 2026 local government elections.

What has Geordin Hill-Lewis said about the coalition talks?

  • Refused to drop Zille, made the DA’s final offer, and calls ActionSA’s demand over her extortion. Story: Joburg council meets to elect a mayor as reports say MK is out of the deal
  • Proposed that the ANC lead 93 hung councils, the DA 24 and the IFP 20, and told 702 the deal’s power is its simplicity. Story: The DA offers the ANC 93 mayoralties; ActionSA rejects its Gauteng deal
  • Wants the party with the most votes to lead each hung council. Story: Should one deal settle all eight metros?

What is Geordin Hill-Lewis’s latest on the coalitions?

On 18 November 2026, EWN: On 702: the DA will not drop Zille. Speaking to Clement Manyathela, Hill-Lewis refused to drop Zille as the DA’s Johannesburg candidate, said the DA would keep working until the first council meetings, and said it would accept opposition if needed.
