Who is Hajra Omarjee?

Hajra Omarjee (Political editor, Business Day) is one of the journalists and commentators Preamble’s Coalitions Tracker follows on the coalition talks after South Africa’s 4 November 2026 local government elections.

What has Hajra Omarjee said about the coalition talks?

  • Reported Luthuli House sources saying Ramaphosa himself told the ANC to treat the DA as a last resort. Story: In public, Ramaphosa asks for good faith. In private, insiders say he wants the DA kept out of Joburg
  • Interviewed Mashaba on ActionSA’s demands about the DA’s mayoral candidates. Story: ActionSA bargains with both big parties, then leans towards the ANC
  • Set out the ANC’s four coalition options, including handing MK some councils. Story: Will the coalition fights break the national unity government?

What is Hajra Omarjee’s latest on the coalitions?

On 23 November 2026, Business Day: Ramaphosa faces tough ANC unity test in coalition talks. Before Monday’s NEC, Omarjee reported that two Luthuli House sources said sidelining the DA for pacts with ActionSA, the EFF and MK had sparked fierce debate, but opponents of the “done deal” expected to be in the minority. The sources said Ramaphosa himself had told the party to avoid coalitions with the DA and to treat one as a last resort.
