Who is Gareth van Onselen?

Gareth van Onselen (Political analyst and pollster) is one of the journalists and commentators Preamble’s Coalitions Tracker follows on the coalition talks after South Africa’s 4 November 2026 local government elections.

What has Gareth van Onselen said about the coalition talks?

  • Estimated the ANC lost about 25 points in black metro voting districts, mostly to MK and ActionSA. Story: The ANC loses its grip on the metros, but keeps two mayors through coalition deals

What is Gareth van Onselen’s latest on the coalitions?

On 10 November 2026, The Common Sense: The ANC is being destroyed by its splinters and allies. Van Onselen estimated the ANC lost about 25 points in predominantly black metro voting districts, with roughly 14 going to MK, 10 to ActionSA and 5 to the DA. The Common Sense, which published his figures, argued that this complicates the ANC’s choice of coalition partners.
