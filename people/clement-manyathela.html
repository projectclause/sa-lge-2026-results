Who is Clement Manyathela?

Clement Manyathela (Presenter, 702) is one of the journalists and commentators Preamble’s Coalitions Tracker follows on the coalition talks after South Africa’s 4 November 2026 local government elections.

What has Clement Manyathela said about the coalition talks?

  • Pressed both sides on air: Mashaba ruled out a DA team with Zille, Hill-Lewis refused to drop her, and he asked Hill-Lewis about the mayoral-swap claims. Story: Joburg council meets to elect a mayor as reports say MK is out of the deal
  • Interviewed Hill-Lewis and Beaumont back to back on 702 about their rival plans. Story: The DA offers the ANC 93 mayoralties; ActionSA rejects its Gauteng deal
  • Unpacked the anatomy of coalition politics on 702 as the talks began. Story: Should one deal settle all eight metros?
  • Discussed with listeners the DA opening coalition talks with the EFF and MK. Story: MK talks to everyone, the DA included, but says it wants like-minded partners
  • In his interview, Mashaba made keeping Moya as mayor a condition of any deal. Story: ActionSA’s condition for any deal: Moya stays in Tshwane

What is Clement Manyathela’s latest on the coalitions?

On 18 November 2026, EWN: DA refuses to drop Helen Zille as ActionSA edges closer to an ANC deal. Geordin Hill-Lewis told Manyathela the DA would not drop Zille as its Joburg candidate and would accept opposition if it came to that. ActionSA chair Michael Beaumont called the ANC’s offer attractive and said the DA had never really held talks with ActionSA.
