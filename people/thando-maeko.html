Who is Thando Maeko?

Thando Maeko (Political reporter, Business Day) is one of the journalists and commentators Preamble’s Coalitions Tracker follows on the coalition talks after South Africa’s 4 November 2026 local government elections.

What has Thando Maeko said about the coalition talks?

  • Reported the ANC’s offer to make Mashaba mayor and bypass the DA, and that MK could take Joburg’s deputy mayor post. Story: Joburg council meets to elect a mayor as reports say MK is out of the deal
  • Reported that legal challenges and party disputes were delaying council inaugurations. Story: Outside the metros, councils start to pick partners, and Limpopo’s ANC turns to the DA
  • Reported the ANC NEC weighing competing KwaZulu-Natal proposals, one pairing the ANC, IFP, DA and a ratepayers’ association in eThekwini. Story: MK talks to everyone, the DA included, but says it wants like-minded partners

What is Thando Maeko’s latest on the coalitions?

On 17 November 2026, Business Day: ANC offers ActionSA mayorship deal to bypass DA. Maeko reported that the ANC had proposed a coalition making Herman Mashaba mayor of Johannesburg, keeping Nasiphi Moya in Tshwane and giving the ANC Ekurhuleni, leaving the DA out.
