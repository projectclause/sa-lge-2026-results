Polling

Six organisations published voting-intention polls between the May 2024 general election and the 4 November 2026 local elections. Here is every one, and how the final polls compared with the result.

How the polls did

  • They called the ANC almost exactly. The three final national polls had it on 30% to 32%. It got 30.76%.
  • They missed ActionSA’s surge. Nationally they had it on 3% to 4%; it won 5.67%. In the metros the miss was far larger: 8% to 12% in Tshwane against a result of 24.4%, 7% to 11% in Johannesburg against 16.6%, and 6.1% in Ekurhuleni against 20.3%.
  • They overstated the EFF and MK. The EFF polled 8% to 12.2% and won 6.22%. MK polled 12% to 14.7% and won 9.77%; in eThekwini, its stronghold, polls had it on 43% to 48% and it won 39.7%.
  • They had the ANC too high in the cities. In Cape Town the final polls had it on 19% to 24%; it won 11.9%. In Nelson Mandela Bay, MarkData had the ANC level with the DA on 37%; the DA won 44.3% to the ANC’s 25.8%.
  • The DA was close nationally but underestimated where it is strongest. National polls had it on 25% to 28% against 24.64%, but Cape Town polls had 51% to 56% against 59.4%, and Johannesburg polls 29% to 36% against 39.3%.

Final polls are each organisation’s last poll before the vote. Results are the IEC’s proportional-representation (party) vote. Polls report shares of people who named a party, so they leave out undecided voters and turnout, which was 52%.

About these polls

Source. The figures come from the tables compiled on Wikipedia, not from each pollster’s own release. Check a figure against the original before quoting it.

Methods differ. Ipsos reports registered voters who named a party and does not list parties under 2% separately. The Social Research Foundation’s fieldwork is done by Victory Research and weighted to the registered voter population. A dash means the pollster did not report a figure.

Fake polls. Africa Check debunked a viral graphic showing the ANC on 43% in Tshwane, from an unverified organisation. It is not included.
