An Extremely Detailed Map of the 2026 Local Elections

Explore results from all 4,468 wards in South Africa’s Nov. 4 local elections.

By Preamble · Nov. 23, 2026

    Every ward in the country elected its own councillor on Nov. 4. This map shows who won each one, shaded by how big the win was, so it is possible to see the election street by street: the DA’s hold on Cape Town and the suburbs of Gauteng’s metros, the ANC’s reach across rural South Africa, and MK’s sweep through much of KwaZulu-Natal.

    Search for an address or place to find your ward, or use your location. Zoom in to see individual wards, and tap or hover over a ward to see every candidate who ran.

    How to read the map

    Each ward is coloured by the party of the winning ward candidate. Darker shades mean a larger margin of victory, the gap between the winner and the runner-up as a share of all valid votes. Grey wards have no results in the data. Switch to Turnout to see the share of registered voters who cast a ballot in each ward.

    About the data

    Ward results are from the Independent Electoral Commission’s final results, declared on Nov. 8, 2026. Ward boundaries are simplified so the map loads quickly. Some wards list only their leading candidates; for those, the total vote is estimated from the winner’s share. The ward ballot elects half of each council; seats are then topped up from party lists using the proportional ballot, which is not shown here.

    How we made this map

    Addresses and places are found with OpenStreetMap and matched to the ward that contains them. If you use your location, it stays in your browser and is used only to find your ward.
