Privacy & cookies

The short version: we don’t track you. There are no advertising or analytics trackers on this site and no tracking cookies. The only personal information we receive is what you choose to type into our sign-up form.

Last updated 25 November 2026. This notice is for South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Cookies and trackers

This site does not set any cookies. We don’t use Google Analytics, advertising pixels, heatmaps or any other tracking tools, so there is no cookie banner to click through.

What your browser keeps (not sent to us)

To remember your settings, the site saves a few small items in your own browser’s storage (“local storage” and “session storage”). They stay on your device and are not sent to us.

  • pre-saved: the Coalitions Tracker stories you’ve saved.
  • pre-signed: that you’ve signed up, so we don’t ask again.
  • pre-me: your email address, and your name and surname if you gave them, so the sign-up form isn’t shown to you again.
  • pre-signup-later: when you closed the sign-up box, so it waits a week before asking again.
  • pre-start (this visit only): when your visit started, so the sign-up box waits a minute before it appears.
  • nt-closed (this visit only): that you closed the notification card showing what commentators are saying.

Items marked “this visit only” are deleted when you close the tab. To delete the rest, clear this site’s data in your browser (usually under Settings, then Privacy, then “Cookies and site data” or “Website data”) or use a private window.

The sign-up form

If you sign up, we receive:

  • your email address;
  • your name and surname, if you sign up to save a story (that form asks for them);
  • the page you signed up on, whether you were on a phone or a computer (worked out from your screen width), and the date and time.

This is stored in Preamble’s Google Sheet, sent there through Google Apps Script (script.google.com). That means Google holds the data for us, and it may be stored on Google’s servers outside South Africa. We use it only to send you Preamble updates.

To unsubscribe, or to see, correct or delete what we hold about you, email preamblehq@gmail.com.

Other services your browser contacts

Like any website, loading these pages means your browser connects to the servers below. They see your IP address and basic browser details as part of a normal web request.

  • Netlify hosts the site (preamble.co.za), including the election data files the pages load.
  • Google Fonts provides the typeface (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com).
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) provides the code library that draws the charts and maps on the results and map pages (cdnjs.cloudflare.com).
  • OpenStreetMap: only if you search for an address on the ward map, what you typed is sent to OpenStreetMap’s search service (nominatim.openstreetmap.org) to find the place.
  • Google Apps Script: only when you sign up (see above).

The share buttons only open WhatsApp, Facebook, X and the other services when you tap them, and links to news reports take you to those publishers’ own sites, which have their own privacy policies.

Your rights

Under POPIA you can:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct it or delete it;
  • object to us using it, including for updates (you can unsubscribe at any time);
  • complain to the Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za.
