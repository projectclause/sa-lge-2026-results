Who will run South Africa’s metros? Coalition talks after the 2026 local elections

Updated 24 November 2026, 09:00

Stories

Joburg will elect its mayor on Friday as parties finish their dealSouth Africa · Johannesburg · 24 November 2026All 270 councillors were sworn in on Monday, but the election of a mayor, deputy mayor and speaker now waits until Friday, 27 November. The ANC, ActionSA, EFF and MK deal that would make Mashaba mayor is in its final stages but still to be signed.

Should one deal settle all eight metros?South Africa · National · 24 November 2026Instead of eight separate fights, Sizwe Mpofu-Walsh floated a single bargain between the DA, ANC and ActionSA over the metro mayors. Others have pitched their own national formulas.

MK talks to everyone, the DA included, but says it wants like-minded partnersSouth Africa · MK Party · 24 November 2026MK came third nationally and leads many KwaZulu-Natal councils. Its negotiator Dali Mpofu has met the DA for the first time while warning the ANC about the partners it chooses.

South Africa’s cities move to coalition ruleInternational · Metros · 23 November 2026Straight after the results, Bloomberg and Reuters reported that the biggest cities would be run by coalitions, and AP described high-stakes talks after the ANC’s worst result. Two weeks on, Bloomberg’s S’thembile Cele wrote that post-election bartering was counting for more than votes, while the FT and the BBC reported the ANC fighting to keep control of key cities.

ActionSA bargains with both big parties, then leans towards the ANCSouth Africa · Gauteng metros · 23 November 2026ActionSA’s chair Michael Beaumont spent November talking to both the DA and the ANC, arguing that coalitions, not vote share, decide who governs.

In public, Ramaphosa asks for good faith. In private, insiders say he wants the DA kept out of JoburgSouth Africa · National · 23 November 2026The president told all parties to bargain in good faith and form councils quickly. A report based on unnamed insiders says he steered the ANC away from the DA in Johannesburg, which the ANC denies.

Outside the metros, councils start to pick partners, and Limpopo’s ANC turns to the DASouth Africa · Beyond the metros · 23 November 2026With deadlines close, deals are being struck in smaller councils. In Limpopo, the ANC chose the DA over a left-wing bloc, while the IFP expects to seal its KwaZulu-Natal deals this weekend.

The DA offers the ANC 93 mayoralties; ActionSA rejects its Gauteng dealSouth Africa · National · 23 November 2026The DA’s nationwide framework would have the ANC lead 93 hung councils, the DA 24 and the IFP 20. A separate Gauteng offer, which ActionSA rejected, would have let ActionSA run Tshwane while the DA led Johannesburg and Ekurhuleni.

The ANC turns down ActionSA’s offer of council seatsSouth Africa · Gauteng · 23 November 2026The ANC won no PR seats in Gauteng’s three metros, which could keep some of its regional leaders out of council. ActionSA offered to bring ANC members in through its own lists. A senior Gauteng ANC source told The Citizen the answer is no.

ActionSA’s condition for any deal: Moya stays in TshwaneSouth Africa · Tshwane · 23 November 2026ActionSA made keeping Nasiphi Moya as Tshwane mayor a condition for any coalition, and News24’s Adriaan Basson expected her back.

Will the coalition fights break the national unity government?South Africa · National · 22 November 2026Adriaan Basson first expected the DA and ANC to copy their national partnership in the councils. By mid-November he was warning that the fight over the cities could bring it down.

A turning point for Mandela’s partyInternational · Analysis · 22 November 2026Many foreign pieces treat the result as a turning point for the ANC. The New York Times’ John Eligon wrote that South Africa’s politics may never be the same again. The Economist welcomed voters giving up on the party, AP explained how it lost its way, and the FT reported on townships turning against it.

The ANC loses its grip on South Africa’s metrosSouth Africa · Metros · 21 November 2026News24’s columnists saw the ANC’s decline speeding up, and after the count it was left without control of a single metro.

Abroad, Johannesburg is the story: a deal to shut out the DAInternational · Johannesburg · 20 November 2026Johannesburg gets the most attention outside South Africa. Reuters and the FT reported that the ANC and its partners were close to a deal that would sideline the DA, which won the most votes in the city. The FT reported that Helen Zille had told allies her bid for mayor was over, and The Africa Report called Herman Mashaba the kingmaker in Gauteng.

What the city deals mean for the unity governmentInternational · National · 20 November 2026Foreign outlets keep asking whether the local fights will spill into the national unity government. Bloomberg reported that the DA would stay in the GNU for now. Semafor’s Sam Mkokeli called the metros an urban test for it, Justice Malala argued in Bloomberg Opinion that the coalition government has to succeed, and Bloomberg’s Next Africa podcast asked whether it could collapse.

The rand slides as investors watch the talksInternational · Markets · 19 November 2026Financial outlets link the talks to the currency. Reuters reported the rand at a seven-month low on 17 November as traders tracked the coalition talks, and near a one-year low two days later. Bloomberg reported it testing R17 to the dollar with the focus on politics, and Michael Cohen wrote that the messy fallout had rattled investors.
