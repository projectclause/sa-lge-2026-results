The ANC leaves coalition choices to its provinces and regionsSouth Africa · ANC · 25 November 2026EWN and Dan Corder report the ANC’s NEC has let provinces and regions decide coalitions, with consequences for Gauteng, eThekwini, Nelson Mandela Bay and over 100 hung councils. The ANC says there is consensus as talks move towards conclusion, while TimesLIVE and IOL report strains over Ramaphosa’s leadership.

The DA is shut out of Theewaterskloof and Laingsburg as Western Cape councils pick partnersSouth Africa · Western Cape · 25 November 2026News24 reports the ANC joined other parties to take control of Theewaterskloof, and IOL reports ActionSA formed a Laingsburg coalition with the PA and ANC, leaving the DA out. News24 reports the DA and FF Plus have entered coalitions in some hung Western Cape councils.

Ekurhuleni’s council meets to elect its leaders as Mashaba confirms a deputy mayorSouth Africa · Ekurhuleni · 25 November 2026Ekurhuleni’s new council held its inaugural meeting on 25 November, with IOL reporting Kagiso Lerutla presided. IOL reports Herman Mashaba confirmed Xolani Khumalo as Ekurhuleni deputy mayor.

Malema takes Zille to court over her Joburg claimsSouth Africa · Johannesburg · 25 November 2026EFF leader Julius Malema went to court to stop DA mayoral candidate Helen Zille repeating claims about him, in a dispute linked to the Johannesburg coalition fight, and IOL reports he threatened R1m defamation action. Zille refused to retract, and the court reserved judgment.

Faku returns in Buffalo City with the EFF as a DA-led coalition signs for Nelson Mandela BaySouth Africa · Eastern Cape · 25 November 2026Princess Faku was re-elected Buffalo City mayor, beating the UDM’s Nqabayomzi Kwankwa, and BusinessTech reports the ANC teamed up with the EFF. In Nelson Mandela Bay, a DA-led multiparty coalition has been formed, but News24 reports an ANC-led filibuster blocked the vote for mayor and speaker.

eThekwini’s first council sitting ends without a mayor as the ANC and MK are reported to have a dealSouth Africa · eThekwini · 25 November 2026eThekwini’s inaugural council meeting adjourned on 24 November without electing a mayor, deputy mayor or speaker, to give parties time to finish coalition deals. IOL and Briefly News report the ANC and MK have reached an eThekwini agreement, though the parties were coy at the sitting.

The ANC loses its grip on South Africa’s metrosSouth Africa · Metros · 25 November 2026News24’s columnists saw the ANC’s decline speeding up, and after the count it was left without control of a single metro.

Will the coalition fights break the national unity government?South Africa · National · 25 November 2026Adriaan Basson first expected the DA and ANC to copy their national partnership in the councils. By mid-November he was warning that the fight over the cities could bring it down.

ActionSA’s condition for any deal: Moya stays in TshwaneSouth Africa · Tshwane · 25 November 2026ActionSA made keeping Nasiphi Moya as Tshwane mayor a condition for any coalition, and News24’s Adriaan Basson expected her back.

ActionSA bargains with both big parties, then leans towards the ANCSouth Africa · Gauteng metros · 25 November 2026ActionSA’s chair Michael Beaumont spent November talking to both the DA and the ANC, arguing that coalitions, not vote share, decide who governs.

MK talks to everyone, the DA included, but says it wants like-minded partnersSouth Africa · MK Party · 25 November 2026MK came third nationally and leads many KwaZulu-Natal councils. Its negotiator Dali Mpofu has met the DA for the first time while warning the ANC about the partners it chooses.

Should one deal settle all eight metros?South Africa · National · 25 November 2026Instead of eight separate fights, Sizwe Mpofu-Walsh floated a single bargain between the DA, ANC and ActionSA over the metro mayors. Others have pitched their own national formulas.

Outside the metros, councils start to pick partners, and Limpopo’s ANC turns to the DASouth Africa · Beyond the metros · 25 November 2026With deadlines close, deals are being struck in smaller councils. In Limpopo, the ANC chose the DA over a left-wing bloc, while the IFP expects to seal its KwaZulu-Natal deals this weekend.

The DA offers the ANC 93 mayoralties; ActionSA rejects its Gauteng dealSouth Africa · National · 25 November 2026The DA’s nationwide framework would have the ANC lead 93 hung councils, the DA 24 and the IFP 20. A separate Gauteng offer, which ActionSA rejected, would have let ActionSA run Tshwane while the DA led Johannesburg and Ekurhuleni.

Joburg will elect its mayor on Friday as parties finish their dealSouth Africa · Johannesburg · 25 November 2026All 270 councillors were sworn in on Monday, but the election of a mayor, deputy mayor and speaker now waits until Friday, 27 November. The ANC, ActionSA, EFF and MK deal that would make Mashaba mayor is in its final stages but still to be signed.

The DA secures a five-party coalition in Emfuleni after a sitting collapsesSouth Africa · Beyond the metros · 24 November 2026ANC, EFF and MK councillors disrupted Emfuleni’s mayoral election sitting, and Nova News reports it collapsed. News24 reports the DA has secured a five-party coalition to take control of the municipality, giving up posts to do so.

The ANC turns down ActionSA’s offer of council seatsSouth Africa · Gauteng · 24 November 2026The ANC won no PR seats in Gauteng’s three metros, which could keep some of its regional leaders out of council. ActionSA offered to bring ANC members in through its own lists. A senior Gauteng ANC source told The Citizen the answer is no.

South Africa’s cities move to coalition ruleInternational · Metros · 23 November 2026Straight after the results, Bloomberg and Reuters reported that the biggest cities would be run by coalitions, and AP described high-stakes talks after the ANC’s worst result. Two weeks on, Bloomberg’s S’thembile Cele wrote that post-election bartering was counting for more than votes, while the FT and the BBC reported the ANC fighting to keep control of key cities.

MK members dispute the party’s PR councillor listsSouth Africa · MK Party · 23 November 2026The Witness and IOL report the MK Party faces internal discontent and a backlash over its KwaZulu-Natal proportional representation list. The Daily Dispatch reports the party’s Port St Johns branch is threatening court action over a contested PR councillor list.

In public, Ramaphosa asks for good faith. In private, insiders say he wants the DA kept out of JoburgSouth Africa · National · 23 November 2026The president told all parties to bargain in good faith and form councils quickly. A report based on unnamed insiders says he steered the ANC away from the DA in Johannesburg, which the ANC denies.

A turning point for Mandela’s partyInternational · Analysis · 22 November 2026Many foreign pieces treat the result as a turning point for the ANC. The New York Times’ John Eligon wrote that South Africa’s politics may never be the same again. The Economist welcomed voters giving up on the party, AP explained how it lost its way, and the FT reported on townships turning against it.

What the city deals mean for the unity governmentInternational · National · 20 November 2026Foreign outlets keep asking whether the local fights will spill into the national unity government. Bloomberg reported that the DA would stay in the GNU for now. Semafor’s Sam Mkokeli called the metros an urban test for it, Justice Malala argued in Bloomberg Opinion that the coalition government has to succeed, and Bloomberg’s Next Africa podcast asked whether it could collapse.

Abroad, Johannesburg is the story: a deal to shut out the DAInternational · Johannesburg · 20 November 2026Johannesburg gets the most attention outside South Africa. Reuters and the FT reported that the ANC and its partners were close to a deal that would sideline the DA, which won the most votes in the city. The FT reported that Helen Zille had told allies her bid for mayor was over, and The Africa Report called Herman Mashaba the kingmaker in Gauteng.

The rand slides as investors watch the talksInternational · Markets · 19 November 2026Financial outlets link the talks to the currency. Reuters reported the rand at a seven-month low on 17 November as traders tracked the coalition talks, and near a one-year low two days later. Bloomberg reported it testing R17 to the dollar with the focus on politics, and Michael Cohen wrote that the messy fallout had rattled investors.
