Who will run South Africa’s metros? Coalition talks after the 2026 local elections

Updated 27 November 2026, 18:10

Stories

MK and the ANC take uMhlathuze, Newcastle and Msunduzi as the ANC, DA and IFP take KwaDukuzaSouth Africa · Beyond the metros · 27 November 2026IOL, News24 and SABC News report the MK Party and ANC have taken uMhlathuze, with Nomathamsanqa Mthembu elected mayor, and The Witness reports the two parties have formed a coalition to govern Msunduzi. Tabloid Newspapers reports MK’s Stuart Zwane has been elected Newcastle mayor, while The Citizen reports a last-minute deal put the ANC, DA and IFP in control of KwaDukuza.

Geordin Hill-Lewis is re-elected Cape Town mayor and names his mayoral committeeSouth Africa · Cape Town · 27 November 2026IOL reports Geordin Hill-Lewis has been re-elected mayor of Cape Town, pledging jobs and stronger policing. The City of Cape Town, The Bulrushes and EWN report he has announced a new mayoral committee, and ActionSA says it will hold the new members accountable.

The ANC leaves coalition choices to its provinces and regionsSouth Africa · ANC · 27 November 2026EWN and Dan Corder report the ANC’s NEC has let provinces and regions decide coalitions, affecting over 100 hung councils, and SABC News and IOL report the NEC resolved that DA coalitions should be a last resort. The Citizen reports Fikile Mbalula said 'the ANC did not win this election' as the party sealed coalition agreements.

The DA takes Knysna and Bitou but is shut out of Theewaterskloof, Laingsburg and LangebergSouth Africa · Western Cape · 27 November 2026News24 reports the ANC joined other parties to take control of Theewaterskloof, IOL and the Cape Times report ActionSA formed a Laingsburg coalition with the PA and ANC, and the Cape Argus reports an ANC-PA coalition won Langeberg, all leaving the DA out. The Cape Argus reports the DA has taken Knysna and Bitou, and EWN reports the Knysna deal is with the FF Plus and the Knysna Civic Alliance.

The ANC’s Mziyanda Mketsu is elected Ekurhuleni mayor in a deal with ActionSA and the EFFSouth Africa · Ekurhuleni · 27 November 2026The Citizen and IOL report the ANC’s Mziyanda Mketsu has been elected executive mayor of Ekurhuleni, and BusinessTech reports the ANC has taken the metro with ActionSA and the EFF. IOL, Business Day, EWN and Inside Politic report the EFF’s Nthabiseng Tshivhenga was re-elected council speaker.

A court bars Zille from repeating her claims about MalemaSouth Africa · Johannesburg · 27 November 2026IOL, News24 and Central News South Africa report a court has barred DA mayoral candidate Helen Zille from repeating her claims about EFF leader Julius Malema, including that he controlled Herman Mashaba and influenced Shadrack Sibiya’s Johannesburg appointment. Sunday World calls it Malema’s first-round win in his defamation lawsuit against Zille.

Faku returns in Buffalo City with the EFF as the DA’s Retief Odendaal returns as Nelson Mandela Bay mayorSouth Africa · Eastern Cape · 27 November 2026Princess Faku was re-elected Buffalo City mayor, beating the UDM’s Nqabayomzi Kwankwa, and BusinessTech and The South African report the ANC teamed up with the EFF. In Nelson Mandela Bay, IOL, EWN, News24 and Daily Maverick report the DA’s Retief Odendaal has been elected mayor in a multiparty coalition deal after an earlier ANC-led filibuster blocked the vote.

MK’s Mxolisi Phakathi is elected eThekwini mayor with ANC support, and Thabani Luthuli becomes deputy mayorSouth Africa · eThekwini · 27 November 2026SABC News, News24, The Witness, The National, BusinessTech and others report the MK Party’s Mxolisi Phakathi has been elected eThekwini mayor with ANC help, ending the ANC’s reign in the metro, and IOL reports MK’s Thabani Luthuli has been elected deputy mayor. The ANC’s Thabani Nyawose was re-elected speaker with MKP support, and SABC News reports the DA has confirmed it made a last-minute coalition offer to the ANC.

The ANC loses its grip on South Africa’s metrosSouth Africa · Metros · 27 November 2026News24’s columnists saw the ANC’s decline speeding up, and after the count it was left without control of a single metro.

ActionSA’s condition for any deal: Moya stays in TshwaneSouth Africa · Tshwane · 27 November 2026ActionSA made keeping Nasiphi Moya as Tshwane mayor a condition for any coalition, and News24’s Adriaan Basson expected her back.

ActionSA bargains with both big parties, then leans towards the ANCSouth Africa · Gauteng metros · 27 November 2026ActionSA’s chair Michael Beaumont spent November talking to both the DA and the ANC, arguing that coalitions, not vote share, decide who governs.

MK talks to everyone, the DA included, but says it wants like-minded partnersSouth Africa · MK Party · 27 November 2026MK came third nationally and leads many KwaZulu-Natal councils. Its negotiator Dali Mpofu has met the DA for the first time while warning the ANC about the partners it chooses.

Joburg council meets to elect a mayor as reports say MK is out of the dealSouth Africa · Johannesburg · 27 November 2026Johannesburg’s council reconvened on Friday, 27 November, to elect a mayor, deputy mayor and speaker. News24 reports MK is out as the coalition deal falls into place in Gauteng’s metros, after Sunday World reported a standoff with MK had put the Joburg deal at risk and the Mail & Guardian reported MK had given the ANC a deadline.

The EFF wins its first mayoralty in Lekwa-Teemane as a Madibeng plan sidelines the ANC and ActionSASouth Africa · Beyond the metros · 26 November 2026Newsnote and Central News South Africa report the EFF’s Justice Dabampe has taken charge of Lekwa-Teemane Municipality, giving the party its first-ever mayoralty. The Citizen reports the ANC and ActionSA face exclusion from a Madibeng leadership plan.

MK members dispute the party’s PR councillor lists and candidatesSouth Africa · MK Party · 26 November 2026The Witness and IOL report the MK Party faces internal discontent and a backlash over its KwaZulu-Natal proportional representation list, and Inside Politic reports MK structures are clashing over a KwaZulu-Natal mayoral candidate. The Daily Dispatch reports the party’s Port St Johns branch is threatening court action over a contested PR councillor list.

The DA secures a five-party coalition in Emfuleni after a sitting collapsesSouth Africa · Beyond the metros · 26 November 2026ANC, EFF and MK councillors disrupted Emfuleni’s mayoral election sitting, and Nova News reports it collapsed. News24 reports the DA has secured a five-party coalition to take control of the municipality, and the DA now accuses the Gauteng government of wilful delays.

Will the coalition fights break the national unity government?South Africa · National · 26 November 2026Adriaan Basson first expected the DA and ANC to copy their national partnership in the councils. By mid-November he was warning that the fight over the cities could bring it down.

In public, Ramaphosa asks for good faith. In private, insiders say he wants the DA kept out of JoburgSouth Africa · National · 26 November 2026The president told all parties to bargain in good faith and form councils quickly. A report based on unnamed insiders says he steered the ANC away from the DA in Johannesburg, which the ANC denies.

Outside the metros, councils start to pick partners, and Limpopo’s ANC turns to the DASouth Africa · Beyond the metros · 26 November 2026With deadlines close, deals are being struck in smaller councils. In Limpopo, the ANC chose the DA over a left-wing bloc, while the IFP expects to seal its KwaZulu-Natal deals this weekend.

MK Party promises stable eThekwini governmentSouth Africa · eThekwini · 25 November 20263 outlets are reporting on the coalition talks in eThekwini. Preamble’s editors have not yet summarised this story; the latest reports are listed below.

Should one deal settle all eight metros?South Africa · National · 25 November 2026Instead of eight separate fights, Sizwe Mpofu-Walsh floated a single bargain between the DA, ANC and ActionSA over the metro mayors. Others have pitched their own national formulas.

The DA offers the ANC 93 mayoralties; ActionSA rejects its Gauteng dealSouth Africa · National · 25 November 2026The DA’s nationwide framework would have the ANC lead 93 hung councils, the DA 24 and the IFP 20. A separate Gauteng offer, which ActionSA rejected, would have let ActionSA run Tshwane while the DA led Johannesburg and Ekurhuleni.

The ANC turns down ActionSA’s offer of council seatsSouth Africa · Gauteng · 24 November 2026The ANC won no PR seats in Gauteng’s three metros, which could keep some of its regional leaders out of council. ActionSA offered to bring ANC members in through its own lists. A senior Gauteng ANC source told The Citizen the answer is no.

South Africa’s cities move to coalition ruleInternational · Metros · 23 November 2026Straight after the results, Bloomberg and Reuters reported that the biggest cities would be run by coalitions, and AP described high-stakes talks after the ANC’s worst result. Two weeks on, Bloomberg’s S’thembile Cele wrote that post-election bartering was counting for more than votes, while the FT and the BBC reported the ANC fighting to keep control of key cities.

A turning point for Mandela’s partyInternational · Analysis · 22 November 2026Many foreign pieces treat the result as a turning point for the ANC. The New York Times’ John Eligon wrote that South Africa’s politics may never be the same again. The Economist welcomed voters giving up on the party, AP explained how it lost its way, and the FT reported on townships turning against it.

What the city deals mean for the unity governmentInternational · National · 20 November 2026Foreign outlets keep asking whether the local fights will spill into the national unity government. Bloomberg reported that the DA would stay in the GNU for now. Semafor’s Sam Mkokeli called the metros an urban test for it, Justice Malala argued in Bloomberg Opinion that the coalition government has to succeed, and Bloomberg’s Next Africa podcast asked whether it could collapse.

Abroad, Johannesburg is the story: a deal to shut out the DAInternational · Johannesburg · 20 November 2026Johannesburg gets the most attention outside South Africa. Reuters and the FT reported that the ANC and its partners were close to a deal that would sideline the DA, which won the most votes in the city. The FT reported that Helen Zille had told allies her bid for mayor was over, and The Africa Report called Herman Mashaba the kingmaker in Gauteng.

The rand slides as investors watch the talksInternational · Markets · 19 November 2026Financial outlets link the talks to the currency. Reuters reported the rand at a seven-month low on 17 November as traders tracked the coalition talks, and near a one-year low two days later. Bloomberg reported it testing R17 to the dollar with the focus on politics, and Michael Cohen wrote that the messy fallout had rattled investors.
